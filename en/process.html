INNOVATION PROCESS

How Does Innovation Work?

Innovation is not a single step. It is a continuous journey that starts with understanding the problem and continues through creating, testing, and improving solutions.

01

Understand the Problem

Identify the real problem before thinking about solutions.

02

Understand the User

Study users' needs and the challenges they face.

03

Generate Solutions

Create multiple ideas and choose the most suitable one.

04

Build a Prototype

Create an initial version to test the idea.

05

Test

Test the solution and gather feedback and data.

06

Continuous Improvement

Improve the idea based on results and feedback.

07

Launch

Launch the solution and continue improving it to create greater impact.
