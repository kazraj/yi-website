OUR STORY

The Story Behind YI

YI (Young Innovators) began from the personal journey of a sixteen-year-old innovator who entered the world of innovation and faced many challenges, including limited opportunities, lack of guidance, and having young ideas overlooked. Over time, it became clear that this challenge was not personal but a gap experienced by many young innovators around the world between having an idea and turning it into a real project. YI was founded to bridge this gap by creating an environment that helps young people understand innovation, develop ideas, and transform them into meaningful projects.
