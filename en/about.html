ABOUT YI

The Story Behind YI

A journey that began with a simple idea and evolved into an initiative dedicated to supporting young innovators and empowering them to transform their ideas into real-world projects.

The Beginning

YI (Young Innovators) began from the personal journey of a sixteen-year-old innovator who entered the world of innovation and faced numerous challenges, including limited opportunities, lack of guidance and mentorship, and having young ideas overlooked.

Challenges

Limited Opportunities

Difficulty accessing suitable programs and opportunities for innovators at the beginning of their journey.

Lack of Guidance

The need for clear direction that helps young people understand the steps of innovation and project development.

Ideas Not Taken Seriously

Many promising ideas do not receive sufficient support in their early stages.

Our Vision

To build a global community that empowers young people to understand innovation, develop their ideas, and transform them into projects that create real impact.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between having an idea and being able to implement it through knowledge, community, mentorship, and continuous guidance.
