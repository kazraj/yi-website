Prerequisite

Module 4 – Scientific Research: Build Your Innovation on Knowledge.

Introduction

Now that you've understood the problem, identified your users, and completed your research, it's time to start generating solutions.

Your first idea is rarely your best one. Most ideas evolve through thinking, experimentation, and continuous improvement before becoming practical innovations.

What Will You Learn?

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • Generate ideas using a structured approach.
  • Improve existing ideas.
  • Evaluate different solutions and choose the most suitable one.
  • Avoid common mistakes during the creative thinking process.

Step 1: Don't Wait for the Perfect Idea

Don't expect your first idea to be complete.

Write down every idea that comes to mind, then improve it over time. Many successful innovations started as simple ideas before becoming effective solutions.

Step 2: Think from Different Perspectives

Ask yourself:

  • Can I simplify the solution?
  • Can I combine two ideas?
  • Can I apply a technology from another field?
  • Can I remove an unnecessary step?

Looking at a problem from different perspectives can help you discover new solutions.

Example

If the problem is that students struggle to find books in the school library, possible solutions might include:

  • An interactive library map.
  • QR codes on bookshelves.
  • A robot that guides visitors.

Step 3: Improve Existing Ideas

You don't always have to start from scratch.

Innovation can come from improving an existing solution or using it in a new way that creates greater value for users.

Step 4: Evaluate Your Ideas

Before choosing an idea, ask yourself:

  • Does it solve the problem?
  • Can it be built with the resources available?
  • Does it provide real value for users?

If the answer is yes, your idea is ready for the next stage.

Common Mistakes

  • Waiting for the perfect idea.
  • Rejecting ideas too quickly.
  • Ignoring users' needs.
  • Choosing ideas that are difficult to implement.
  • Holding on to a weak idea instead of improving it.

Module Summary

After completing this module, you should understand that:

  • Ideas develop over time.
  • Looking at problems from different perspectives leads to better solutions.
  • Improving an existing idea can be an innovation in itself.
  • Evaluating an idea before building it saves time and effort.

Next Step

You now have a promising idea.

In the next module, you'll learn how to turn your idea into a prototype that you can test and improve.
